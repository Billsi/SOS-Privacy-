# Privacy Policy for SOS

**Effective date:** September 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the SOS app ("the App", "we", "us") handles information. SOS is a simple two-player grid game, playable against a computer opponent or another person on the same device.

## Summary

SOS itself does not require an account, login, or any personal information to play. However, the App displays advertising through Google AdMob, which collects certain device and advertising data in order to serve ads. Details are below.

## Information Collected Through Advertising

The App shows banner ads served by **Google AdMob**. To deliver and measure these ads, AdMob and its partners may collect:

- Your device's advertising ID (or a similar identifier)
- IP address and general device/network information
- App interaction data related to ads (such as ad views and clicks)

This data is collected and processed by Google, not stored on our own servers. Google may use it to serve ads relevant to your interests, unless you've limited ad personalization in your device settings. You can review how Google handles this data in the [Google AdMob Privacy & Security policy](https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time through your device settings (on Android: **Settings → Privacy → Ads**, or **Settings → Google → Ads**, depending on device).

## Information We Collect Directly

Outside of advertising, we do not collect any personal information. The App does not:

- Require account creation or login
- Collect your name, email address, or contact information
- Access your device's location, contacts, camera, or microphone
- Request access to your device's files, storage, or photos
- Use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs beyond what is described above

Gameplay itself — board state, scores, and settings — is handled locally on your device.

## Other Third-Party Services

The App loads typefaces from Google Fonts to display text. As with any request to a third-party server, Google may receive standard technical information such as your device's IP address as part of delivering the font files.

## Children's Privacy

This App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App serves ads through Google AdMob, ads are configured to be non-personalized / appropriate for a general audience in line with Google's policies for apps not specifically directed at children. If you believe a child has used the App and you have concerns, contact us using the details below.

## Data Retention and Deletion

We do not directly store personal information on our own servers, so there is no user data held by us to retain or delete. For data collected by Google through AdMob, refer to Google's own privacy policy and account controls linked above.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App's functionality changes — for example, if we add new ad formats, analytics, in-app purchases, or account features. Any such changes will be reflected here with an updated effective date, and we will update the App's Data Safety disclosures on Google Play accordingly.
